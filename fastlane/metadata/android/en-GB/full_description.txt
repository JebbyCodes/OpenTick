OpenTick is a free, open-source planner that keeps your tasks, notes, calendar, school timetable and habits in one place. It works fully offline, needs no account, and has no ads, analytics or tracking.

<b>Tasks and planning</b>
• Lists, projects, tags, sub-tasks, priorities and a quick-add that understands "tomorrow 9am #work !high every week until 31 Dec"
• Repeats that can end on a date, with exceptions: move one occurrence to another time, or skip it
• Reminders at any time before a task (minutes to weeks) or at an exact date and time, as many as you want
• Calendar, week and day views, a Gantt chart, an Eisenhower matrix, habits and a focus timer

<b>School timetable</b>
• Periods, classes, rooms and teachers, with room and teacher always visible (long names scroll)
• Holidays shown as coloured bands in the calendar and timetable
• A persistent notification with your current or next lesson, a countdown and the whole day when expanded, with the room as its icon
• Home-screen widgets, including a timetable widget with the coming days

<b>Notes and templates</b>
• Markdown notes with links, embeds and attachments
• Templates with {{variables}}: OpenTick asks you the questions you set (text, number, date, choice, yes/no, or another note as the answer), fills the template in, and lets you copy it, save it as a note or turn it into a task

<b>Never miss a reminder</b>
• Reminders use the system alarm clock and come back after a restart. Quiet hours and a morning summary are optional.

<b>Privacy</b>
• Your data stays on your device. Optional sync goes to a place you choose (WebDAV, Git, S3-compatible storage or a file), and optional AES-256-GCM encryption protects the data, backups and sync files.

<b>Accessibility</b>
• High contrast, bold text, a dyslexia-friendly font, text spacing, greyscale, "do not rely on colour" marks, larger tap targets, a strong focus outline, reduced motion and more.

OpenTick is released into the public domain under the Unlicense.
